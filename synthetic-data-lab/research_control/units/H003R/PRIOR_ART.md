# Prior art boundary for the replication

Checked September 17, 2026. This pass inspected primary abstract pages, not complete papers or supplementary proofs. It supplies an originality warning, not an exhaustive priority search or a reproduction claim.

Segers, Sibuya and Tsukahara, The Empirical Beta Copula, establishes the rank and beta smoothing construction. Existing empirical beta copulas must not be described as this project's invention.

https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.04430

Kojadinovic and Yi, A class of smooth, possibly data adaptive nonparametric copula estimators containing the empirical beta copula, studies smoothing amount and smoothing shape, including data adaptive estimators. Adaptive copula smoothing in general is not new. Equivalence of the project's training neighborhood bandwidth rule to a particular method in this paper has not been established by this abstract inspection.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.10726

Alaa and colleagues, How Faithful is your Synthetic Data?, separates fidelity, diversity and generalization and introduces sample auditing. Distinguishing useful outputs, coverage and copying is established prior art, not a novel principle claimed by this project.

https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/alaa22a.html

The narrow project evidence is a reproducible Covertype utility and proximity tradeoff plus a failure of the current one step bandwidth rule to match central distance geometry despite a near reference fifth percentile rate. Scientific priority of this empirical boundary condition or a proposed repair remains unestablished. A complete mechanism and nearest method comparison is required before any originality claim.
